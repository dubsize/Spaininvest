b) In the course of a contracted service

Depending on the service, we may process:

4. Retention periods

  • Consultation requests with no follow-up: maximum 12 months, then deleted.
  • Service-related documents: kept for the duration of the contractual relationship. Copies held by Mémoria are deleted within 3 months of its end, except documents we must retain by law.
  • Invoicing data: 6 years (Commercial Code) and 4 years for tax purposes.
  • The client's document dossier: it is built and maintained in the client's own storage space (Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive). The client owns it and may revoke our access at any time, immediately ending our ability to view it.

7. Security and storage principle

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures: restricted access, encrypted storage, two-factor authentication on our accounts, and secure deletion of temporary copies.

Mémoria's core principle: our clients' document dossiers are hosted in their own storage space, which they alone own. We access them only by explicit authorisation, revocable at any time and without notice.

8. Your rights

You have the right at any time to: access your data, rectify inaccurate data, request erasure (subject to our legal retention duties), restrict processing, request portability, object to processing, and withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

To exercise these rights, write to info@starallianceconsult.com, enclosing a copy of an identity document. We respond within one month at most.

If you believe your rights have not been respected, you may lodge a complaint with the Spanish supervisory authority, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (www.aepd.es), C/ Jorge Juan 6, 28001 Madrid.
