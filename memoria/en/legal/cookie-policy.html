Cookie policy

Article 22.2 of Law 34/2002 (LSSI-CE) and AEPD cookie guidance

What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file that websites place on your device to remember information about your browsing.

Cookies used on this site

This site is deliberately minimal: we use no advertising cookies, no profiling cookies and no third-party trackers.

CookieTypePurposeDuration
memoria_cookie_consent Technical — necessary Remembers your cookie banner choice so it isn't shown on every visit. 12 months
Hosting provider technical cookies (Netlify) — strictly necessary for the site to function and for form spam protection. They are not used for advertising tracking.
Strictly necessary technical cookies do not require your prior consent, in accordance with article 22.2 of the LSSI-CE. They are essential for the site to work and for delivering the service you request.

Audience measurement — cookie-free

We measure site traffic using an analytics tool that sets no cookies and collects no personal data. It does not track visitors across sites, builds no profiles, and uses neither persistent identifiers nor device fingerprinting.

The data collected is aggregated and anonymous only: number of visits and page views, pages viewed, referring site or search engine, and country, device type and browser in aggregate form.

None of this identifies you. As no cookie is stored on your device and no personal data is processed within the meaning of the GDPR, this does not require your prior consent. We deliberately chose this approach over a conventional advertising analytics tool.

Manage your consent

Managing cookies in your browser

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Blocking technical cookies may affect how parts of the site work.
